How it works

Three steps, and the setup takes about as long as finding your syllabi.

  1. Upload your syllabi and notes

    Add a syllabus for each course, plus any lecture notes or slides you already have.

  2. Get your weekly schedule

    StudyLane lays out what to study each week across all your courses, working around your exams and deadlines.

  3. Study with quizzes and flashcards

    Practice with quizzes and flashcards generated from your own course material, so you study what you will actually be tested on.
